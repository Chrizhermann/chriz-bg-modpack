# Chriz BG Modpack v0.2.0-alpha.15

## New

- **Marek carries the Boomerang Dagger +2 in BG1 (670).** The dagger's only
  BG1 or SoD source was BG1 NPC Project's Kivan quest. Both of Marek's
  creatures now carry one in the backpack: at his first meeting with the party
  in the north-east district of Baldur's Gate, and at the antidote fight in the
  Blushing Mermaid. Only one of them can die, so one dagger drops. It is
  unidentified and not equipped; he keeps fighting with his usual weapons.
  When the Kivan quest already gives the dagger, the component changes
  nothing. See the [Marek guide](../marek-boomerang-dagger.md).

## What you see

Nothing in the game. The installer prints which Marek files got the dagger,
or why nothing was changed:

```text
[Marek Boomerang Dagger] Shipwreck's Coast (BG3100.BCS) already gives the Boomerang Dagger +2, as BG1 NPC Project's Kivan quest does. Nothing was changed.
```

## Install order

- **670** after mods that change Marek's creature files, such as SCS, and
  before mods that read creature items, such as the Item Randomiser. The
  dagger reaches games in which that Marek has not yet appeared.

Everything else is unchanged from alpha.14.

## Testing status

Automated: synthetic WeiDU tests install, reinstall and uninstall 670 on
BG:EE- and EET-shaped games, with vanilla and SCS-shaped Marek files, with and
without the Kivan quest's Shipwreck's Coast block, and with missing, invalid
or full files. Uninstalling restores the files byte for byte. The installer
also ran on copies of original BG:EE files and of an EET game's files with SCS
and BG1 NPC Project.

Not yet tested in a game: the two Marek fights and the drop.
