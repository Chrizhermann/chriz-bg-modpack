# Chriz BG Modpack v0.2.0-alpha.13

## Fixes

- **Campaign XP banking (650/651) now works, on EEex 1.2.0 and v1.3.1.** In
  alpha.11 and alpha.12 no character got a bank account, so with 650 installed
  every permanent XP award was refused: XP stayed unchanged and nothing was
  banked. Two hooks inside `EEex.dll` were never written. The bank now writes
  them itself and refuses to start if any of its hooks is missing. A startup
  failure leaves XP uncapped and unbanked and is shown in the message log once
  per loaded game. A new game's main character now has an account from the
  start. XP refused under alpha.11/12 is not restored. See the
  [banking guide](../campaign-xp-banking.md).
- **Dorn's ambush (135, new, optional):** the ambush cutscene locked the camera
  on Senjak, and only a completed conversation released it. After a stalled
  conversation, leaving the area crashed the game. 135 removes the lock and
  releases it once Senjak turns hostile. See the
  [Dorn ambush guide](../dorn-ambush-camera.md).
- **Party AI Defensive Stance (460, new):** with "Use special abilities" off,
  the party AI no longer enters Defensive Stance. EET's BG1 stance block in the
  default party script lacked that check.
- **Fade and Evandra catch-up (237/238):** an existing catch-up ladder is now
  recognised under EET's `TRIGGER.IDS`, so a second ladder is no longer added.

## New optional choices

- **EET join XP (239):** adds EET's own SoA join-XP ladder and ToB raise to
  joinable NPCs whose scripts lack them. See the
  [EET join-XP guide](../eet-join-xp.md).
- **Chapter-by-chapter XP caps (652, experimental):** an alternative to 651 on
  top of 650. It caps usable XP per chapter across BG1, SoD, SoA and ToB, banks
  the excess and releases it as chapters advance. It also holds Baeloth's
  Larswood entrance until Chapter 5, so his starting XP fits the cap. If another
  mod replaced that scene, the caps still install and the installer warns.

650/651/652 stay optional, experimental and off by default. No collection
selections changed.

## Install order

- **239** after the NPC mods, EET's NPC transitions and NPC customization mods.
- **650** after EET finalization and the companion XP options you use
  (237/238/239/620), then **651** or **652**. Restart through InfinityLoader.
- **135** after mods that change `CUTDN1B.BCS` or `SENJACK.BCS`, such as EET.
- **460** after EET and any mod that adds or changes party AI scripts.

## Testing status

Automated: the full repository suite passes: 2,696 tests with real WeiDU 249,
including installer install/uninstall and the offline native checks. 487 checks
that need unbundled pinned game executables or local game files were skipped.
The built release archive passes its own 20 checks, including 650/651/652
installed from the archive.

In a game, on a disposable copy of the collection's Recommended install
(BG2:EE 2.7.3.0, EEex v1.3.1, EET with Spell Revisions), with chapters set
directly and XP given from the console:

- Alpha.12 first: as reported, no character got a bank account.
- Banking started with every hook written, and characters got accounts.
- 652 capped, banked and released XP at BG1, SoD and SoA chapter steps, kept
  across save and reload.
- 651's SoA cap applied.
- Baeloth did not appear before Chapter 5 and joined at 40,000 XP from Chapter 5.
- 239 worked alone and with 652 in SoA Chapter 1.
- 135: no camera lock in the full or the cut-short conversation, and no crash on
  leaving.
- 460: no Defensive Stance with "Use special abilities" off; with it on, the
  stance was used.
- A save made under alpha.12 loaded with an account and lost no XP.

Not yet tested in a game: manual level-up and the dual-class pages with banking,
multiclass characters, the record-screen bank display, chapters reached through
the story, 239's ToB raise and 237/238.
